01 / Who he is

Born in Jackson, Mississippi, Aaron came to Nashville at ten and grew up in East Nashville, a proud product of Metro Nashville Public Schools. He found his voice on the stage and radio at Tennessee State and has spent more than 15 years in youth development, nonprofit leadership and civic engagement. Today he coaches leaders, trains bodies, helps steward Nashville's biggest public venues, and raises a family in Antioch that keeps him honest. The common thread is simple: show up.

02 / The four pillars

03 / The journey

From Jackson to Nashville to Dr. McGee.

Roots

Jackson to East Nashville

Born in Jackson, Mississippi and raised in East Nashville from age ten. A proud Metro Nashville Public Schools graduate and class president of Maplewood High School's Class of 2005.

TSU

Finding his voice

Bachelor's in Mass Communication from Tennessee State University. The theater stage, the campus radio station and mentors who saw something in him first.

Service

Youth first

YMCA Black Achievers, the EducateME Foundation, then executive director of Youth Life Foundation of Tennessee. Later Vice President of Action, Advocacy and Education at Oasis Center.

2019

A seat at the table

Appointed to the Metro Nashville Sports Authority, an officer since 2023 and today its Secretary-Treasurer. Part of the team that approved the bonds for the new enclosed stadium.

2020

40 Under Forty

Honored by the TSU Young Alumni Network. The same year Fatherhood Is Dope closed out its second season with guests from RL of Next to Bryan Loritts.

2022

Big Leadership Energy

Founds Big Leadership Energy in March, a social enterprise developing leaders through voice and clarity, and begins doctoral study in Higher Education Leadership at TSU.

2024

Graduate Class President

Elected to lead TSU's graduate students. As a University Innovation Fellow he delivers his first international workshop and studies design thinking at the University of Twente.

2025

Setback into purpose

A November 2024 layoff became a turning point. TruFit Athletics Club took a chance on him, and he earned his NASM personal trainer certification. Coach A was born.

2026

Dr. Aaron T. McGee

Featured in Nashville Fit Magazine, published in The HBCU Experience, re-elected Sports Authority Secretary-Treasurer, and conferred his doctorate.

04 / The partnership

Behind every "Dr." is a we.

Faith and family come first. Aaron and his wife, Terece, met in a classroom in 2012 and have built a life rooted in God's purpose, partnership and showing up for each other and their two children.

Terece is a devoted mother and an entrepreneur in her own right. Every degree, board seat and early morning on the hill is a family effort.
