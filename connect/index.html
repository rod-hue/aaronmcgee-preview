Dr. Aaron T. McGee is a leadership educator, civic leader, and founder of Big Leadership Energy, a social enterprise and cultural platform developing leaders through voice, clarity, and authentic presence.

A Nashville native, Dr. McGee holds an Ed.D. in Higher Education Leadership from Tennessee State University, a Master of Arts in Civic Leadership from Lipscomb University, and a Bachelor of Science in Mass Communication. His doctoral research looks at gaps in youth leadership skills and at how co-governance structures in community organizations can co-opt the voices they are meant to elevate.

He has more than 15 years of experience in youth development, nonprofit leadership, and civic engagement. Over that time he has served as Program Director, Executive Director, CEO, Vice President, and Board Chairman across several sectors. He serves on the Nashville Sports Authority Board and works as a Senior Operations Manager at an organization committed to social justice and community organizing.

In March 2022, Dr. McGee founded Big Leadership Energy to help young leaders build self-efficacy, advocate authentically, and define their voice with clarity and purpose. Through BLE, he offers leadership coaching, workshops, and curriculum design to student leaders, emerging professionals, universities, and community organizations in Nashville and beyond.

Big Leadership Energy is also a cultural voice. Drawing on his background in mass communication, Dr. McGee uses social media and storytelling to share the stories of leaders across generations.

Dr. McGee is rooted in faith and servant leadership. He believes real change comes from leaders who understand their own power and use it to serve their communities.
