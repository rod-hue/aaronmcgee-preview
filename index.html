02 / Meet Aaron

Show up. Then build.

Born in Jackson, Mississippi and raised in East Nashville, Aaron is a proud product of Metro Nashville Public Schools who found his voice on the stage and the radio at Tennessee State.

For more than 15 years he has worked in youth development, nonprofit leadership and civic engagement. Today he coaches leaders, trains bodies, serves on the Nashville Sports Authority and, as of 2026, carries a doctorate in Higher Education Leadership. Through every title, faith and family set the pace. His two rules are simple: work with who shows up, and build with what you have.
