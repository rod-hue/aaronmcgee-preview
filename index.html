02 / Meet Aaron

Aaron T. McGee

Born in Jackson, Mississippi and raised in East Nashville, Aaron is a proud product of Metro Nashville Public Schools who found his voice on the stage and the radio at Tennessee State. More than fifteen years later, he is still showing up for the city that raised him.

He coaches leaders, trains bodies, helps steward Nashville's public venues on the Sports Authority, and in 2026 became Dr. McGee. Through every title, faith and family set the pace.
